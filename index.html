Instructions: Applying to Gmail

  1. Customize your personal contact info and check or uncheck which office locations you need.
  2. Click "Copy Signature for Gmail".
  3. Open Gmail, click the Gear icon (Settings) → "See all settings".
  4. Under the General tab, scroll down to the Signature section.
  5. Click "Create new" (or select your existing signature) and press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on Mac) into the editor box.
  6. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click "Save Changes".
