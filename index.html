How to add this signature to Gmail

  1. Fill in your credentials and adjust the stacked office locations as needed.
  2. Click the Copy Signature to Clipboard button.
  3. Open Gmail and click the Settings icon (top right gear) → See all settings.
  4. Scroll down to the Signature section under the General tab.
  5. Create a new signature (or edit an existing one), click inside the text editor box, and press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on Mac).
  6. Scroll to the bottom of the Gmail screen and click Save Changes.
